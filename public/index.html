About Northern Ireland Tech Radar

What is this and why build it?

This started as a personal project to cut through generic job board noise and get a clear, unvarnished picture of Northern Ireland’s tech ecosystem. It tracks indigenous startups, scaleups, FDI tech hubs, and academic commercialisation centres across the region, specifically keeping an eye on open product leadership roles (Product Managers, Group PMs, Directors, and VPs).

How does it work?

A built-in scanner regularly queries public applicant tracking system (ATS) APIs and career feeds to pull verified live vacancies with direct application links.

Automated checks supported on:
Greenhouse Ashby SmartRecruiters Workable Lever Teamtailor BambooHR Pinpoint Volcanic
What's not supported via direct API: Closed enterprise systems like Workday, Taleo, iTrent, or custom portals don't expose open feeds, so they're marked N/A (Portal). Use the 1-click Careers button or the LinkedIn search shortcut to check them directly.

How to contribute or report updates

Know a Northern Ireland tech company that should be on here, spotted an out-of-date link, or want to suggest a new ATS connector? Contributions are very welcome. The entire project is open source and can also be forked and tailored to track any other region, industry vertical, or job discipline.
